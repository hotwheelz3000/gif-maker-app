## GIF Maker 1.0

The first version: turn any video from Photos into a GIF, right on the iPhone.

- **Trim:** thumbnail strip with yellow handles and a time bubble, clips up to 15 seconds. Long videos zoom in to 30 seconds around your selection. Drag inside the selection to slide it, or use the Start and End + / − buttons (hold to keep going).
- **Crop:** Original, 1:1, 3:2, 16:9, 4:5 or 9:16. Drag and pinch the preview, or use the Zoom and Move buttons.
- **Loop:** Normal, Reverse or Boomerang, at 0.5x to 2x speed. Reverse and Boomerang previews play smoothly and match the GIF.
- **Quality:** 320, 480 or 720 pixels wide and 10 to 24 frames per second (720 and 15 by default).
- **Your GIF:** save to Photos, share, or copy.
- **Accessibility:** 44-point buttons, VoiceOver labels and adjustable trim handles, a button for every drag or pinch, and Reduce Motion respected.
- **Cleanup before release:** a review of all the code. Reverse/Boomerang previews stop preparing when you leave the editor or make a GIF (so they don't slow the export), videos too short for a GIF now say so, each GIF gets its own file name, the "can't play backwards" note only shows when it's true, and leftover code from earlier builds is gone.
